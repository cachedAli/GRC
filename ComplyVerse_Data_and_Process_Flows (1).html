About this diagram

This document describes how information moves through the CompliVerse AI platform from a senior leader's point of view. Each numbered flow corresponds to one core business capability — onboarding a tenant, intaking a compliance framework, managing a risk through to approval, processing an item of evidence, triaging a vulnerability finding, asking the assistant a question, running an audit engagement, and dispatching deadline reminders.

Every label, decision, and arrow is written in plain business English. Where a person must explicitly review or approve before automation may continue, that step is marked as a human-in-the-loop gate with a hatched amber background and a "Manual Review" ribbon. Where information is filed for the long term, a labelled cylinder shows the relevant information store. Where a step can fail, a red dashed arrow leads to a clearly labelled fallback outcome.

Use these diagrams alongside the system-architecture deliverable: the architecture explains what the platform is, while these process flows explain how it behaves day-to-day for the people who depend on it.

" Every flow can be traced from the first user action to a recorded outcome — with the human checkpoints, fallback paths and information stores made explicit on a single page. — Design principle, CompliVerse AI
