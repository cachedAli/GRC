# Conversion and CTA strategy

Primary CTA: **Book a demo**. Secondary CTAs: View product tour, View framework library, Explore [module], Discuss architecture, Discuss integration requirement.

Demo form: name, work email, organization, role, primary objective, framework interest and optional message. Do not request phone, budget or timeline initially. Do not promise response times until operationally confirmed.

Early visitors go to Tour/Frameworks; evaluators go to Demo; technical visitors use architecture/topic-prefilled demo form.
