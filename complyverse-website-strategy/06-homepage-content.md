# Homepage content

## Page name

CompliVerse homepage

### Page purpose

Explain the connected product model and convert qualified visitors.

### Target audience

Compliance, risk, security and audit leaders.

### Main message

Turn framework requirements into connected control, evidence and risk work.

### Section order and full copy

#### Hero

* Headline: **Turn framework requirements into connected GRC work.**
* Supporting copy: Map requirements to reusable controls, assign ownership, link supporting evidence, track risk and actions, and bring the record together for review and reporting.
* CTA: **Book a demo**. Secondary: **View product tour**.
* Visual: real control/evidence relationship view.

#### Operating model

* Headline: **A requirement should lead somewhere useful.**
* Copy: Start with a framework or uploaded programme. Organize controls, connect evidence and risk, route review work, then use reports and audit packages to understand progress.

#### Product areas

* Headline: **Work across the records that matter.**
* Blocks: Controls & evidence; Enterprise risk & RCSA; Governance & policy; Third-party risk.

#### Frameworks

* Headline: **Start from the frameworks in your programme.**
* Copy: The runtime catalog contains 33 non-fictional framework/control libraries. Availability and programme scope require confirmation.

#### AI

* Headline: **Use assistance where judgement still matters.**
* Copy: Assisted drafting, mapping, evidence assessment, risk work and chat are partial capabilities. Responsible teams review outputs.

#### Final CTA

* Headline: **See the workflow with your programme in mind.**
* CTA: Book a demo.

### Required assets

Approved screenshots, framework availability statement, security review.
