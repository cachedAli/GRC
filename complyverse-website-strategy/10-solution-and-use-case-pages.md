# Solution and use-case pages

Organize by role and operational problem, not company size.

- Compliance teams: frameworks → controls → evidence → review/report.
- Risk teams: risks → controls/assets/incidents → treatment/KRI/review.
- Security teams: asset/vulnerability/control context; later and qualified.
- Internal audit: controls/evidence/packages/review.
- Audit readiness: version, link, review and package evidence.
- Multi-framework programmes: normalized controls and mappings.
- Evidence operations: request, version, link, review and package proof.

Each page uses problem → current manual process → confirmed workflow → real screens → FAQ → demo. Company-stage pages wait for team interviews.
