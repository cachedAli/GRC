# Visual content strategy

Use an operational-ledger direction and the Evidence Thread described in `26-reference-consolidation-and-design-direction.md`. Show actual screenshots, framework/control maps, evidence timelines, risk-control relationships, policy approvals, vendor assessment stages, report previews and permission diagrams.

Never fabricate readiness values, customer names, risk scores, integration states or AI confidence. Motion should explain relationships, respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid ambient futuristic decoration. Detailed screenshot comparison is in `25-screenshot-visual-analysis.md`.
