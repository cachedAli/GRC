# Product module pages

## Controls & evidence

Hero: **Make control work traceable from requirement to evidence.** Workflow: framework → mapped control → owner/test → evidence version → review/package. Capabilities: normalized/common controls, workbench, tests, evidence versions, mappings, coverage and audit packages.

## Enterprise risk & RCSA

Hero: **Keep risk decisions connected to the work behind them.** Workflow: risk → assessment → linked controls/assets/evidence → KRI/mitigation/review → report. Include RCSA templates, campaigns, responses, findings and approvals.

## Governance & policy

Hero: **Give policy work a record of ownership, review and decision.** Workflow: draft → version → review → approval/signoff → attestation → history. Include regulatory changes, committees, exceptions and mappings carefully.

## Third-party risk

Hero: **Make third-party review a lifecycle, not an inbox.** Workflow: intake → tiering → questionnaire/evidence → findings/remediation → approval → monitoring/offboarding. External monitoring sources require confirmation.

Security operations, workflow automation, reporting/audit, BCM, projects/tasks and issues remain combined/later pages to avoid thin or premature product pages.
