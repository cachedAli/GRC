# Reference consolidation and CompliVerse design direction

## What the combined references say

The six live sites and supplied screenshots converge on five strategic behaviours:

1. Broad GRC products need grouped navigation, not a flat feature list.
2. Framework-led visitors need a direct route from the header.
3. Mature vendors use customer proof to support broad claims.
4. AI is explained through named tasks or agents rather than a single generic paragraph.
5. Conversion actions remain visible while visitors explore dense menus.

CompliVerse can use the first, second, fourth and fifth behaviours. It cannot use the third until customer evidence exists.

## Distinctive design plan

### Subject, audience and job

Subject: the operational record behind a GRC programme. Audience: compliance, risk, security and audit leaders. Single job: help a qualified visitor understand how framework requirements become owned, reviewable work and choose a product tour or demo.

### Colour tokens

- **Ledger Ink** `#12202B`: primary text and navigation.
- **Control Blue** `#2457D6`: links, selected entities and primary actions.
- **Evidence Teal** `#0E7C74`: evidence and verified-state accent.
- **Review Amber** `#C17817`: pending review and careful-attention state.
- **Paper Mist** `#F4F7F8`: calm page background.
- **Register White** `#FFFFFF`: cards and content surfaces.

This avoids Secureframe’s mint identity and Drata’s space-dark identity while staying appropriate for enterprise records.

### Typography

- Display: **Instrument Sans** or another moderately condensed humanist sans, used for page theses and menu-group titles.
- Body/UI: **Source Sans 3**, chosen for long-form legibility and dense product terminology.
- Data/captions: **IBM Plex Mono**, used sparingly for framework versions, control identifiers and status labels. It encodes real system data rather than borrowing Drata’s decorative command syntax.

Font availability and licensing must be confirmed during build.

### Layout concept

Use a ledger-like 12-column grid. Major content follows a visible evidence thread from left to right. Dividers represent actual changes in record ownership or lifecycle state. Cards are reserved for independent choices; sequential workflow steps use a connected track instead.

### Signature element

The **Evidence Thread**: a single interactive line that starts at a framework requirement and passes through control, owner, evidence, risk/action and review. Hover or keyboard focus reveals the connected product screen and explanation. This is the one visual risk worth taking because it is specific to the product’s confirmed relationship model.

### Self-critique and revision

An earlier dark “command center” direction would resemble Drata and make CompliVerse look more futuristic than the product evidence supports. A bright green compliance aesthetic would resemble Secureframe. The revised ledger direction is calmer and more defensible: it uses the vocabulary of controls, registers, evidence and review states without looking like a spreadsheet.

## Final navigation implication

- **Platform**: Overview, Product tour, AI assistance, Security and architecture.
- **Products**: Controls & evidence, Enterprise risk & RCSA, Governance & policy, Third-party risk.
- **Solutions**: initially Product tour, Multi-framework programmes and Audit preparation; role pages added later.
- **Frameworks**: verified shortlist plus View all frameworks.
- **Resources**: Framework library and product documentation only when maintained.
- **Company**: About and Contact.
- Utilities: Login and Book a demo.

Do not launch Customers, Partners, Pricing, Trust Center, Careers, Newsroom, Academy, Marketplace or status links until each destination and operational promise is real.
