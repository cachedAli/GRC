CompliVerse is a tenant-aware GRC application that connects framework controls, evidence, risk, governance workflows, vendor risk and security operations records.

## Value pillars

1. **Connected compliance records.** Problem: controls, evidence and risk sit in separate registers. Capability: explicit cross-links among controls, evidence, risk, assets, documents, issues and vendors. Outcome: a traceable operating view. Proof: `models/_08`–`_14`, `_35`, `_41`.
2. **Operational assessment and remediation.** Problem: assessments do not become accountable actions. Capability: ERM/RCSA, mitigation actions, findings, approvals and workflow engine. Outcome: work can be routed and reviewed. Proof: `_11`, `_26`, `_34`.
3. **Audit and governance discipline.** Problem: policies/evidence are hard to version and prepare. Capability: document versions/approvals/signoffs, evidence versions and audit packages. Proof: `_10`, `_13`.
4. **Security context for GRC.** Problem: programme data lacks asset/vulnerability context. Capability: asset, discovery, vulnerability and compliance-plugin surfaces. Proof: `_14`, `_22`, `_37`, `_47`; qualify as partial.

## Connected platform story

`Select or upload a framework → organize and map controls → assign/control work → collect and link evidence → assess risk and actions → review/approve → build audit packages and reports.`

The framework story may say that the runtime catalog contains 33 non-fictional framework/control catalogs, but should not promise universal commercial availability. Recommended CTAs: **Book a demo**, **Explore the platform**, **View framework library**, **Talk to a compliance specialist**. Do not claim trials, pricing or downloadable assessments.
