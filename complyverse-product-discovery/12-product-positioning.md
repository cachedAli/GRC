# Product positioning grounded in code

## Category and ICP interpretation

Primary category: configurable GRC and cyber-risk operations platform. Secondary categories: compliance/control management, ERM/RCSA, governance/policy management, third-party risk, vulnerability/asset posture management, audit readiness and workflow automation.

Best-fit interpretation: organisations with a compliance programme spanning multiple frameworks, control owners, evidence collection, risk/action tracking and auditors. The runtime catalog spans Gulf, UAE, Saudi, Qatar, Pakistan, Singapore, Sri Lanka, EU, US and global sources, and NCA/PDPL paths add dedicated regional evidence. Commercial target geography and industries are nevertheless unconfirmed.

## Jobs and outcomes supported by evidence

- Structure framework/control work and cross-framework mappings.
- Link evidence, risks, assets, documents, issues and vendors instead of operating separate registers.
- Run assessment, review, approval, attestation and remediation workflows.
- Provide audits, work queues, dashboards and reports.
- Support security teams with asset, vulnerability, CIS/plugin and risk-posture surfaces.

## Differentiators worth exploring

The unusually broad linkage model across compliance, ERM, governance, vendor risk and security operations is the strongest documented product story. Other notable evidence is the configurable workflow engine, regional NCA/SAMA/DCC material, and AI usage/budget instrumentation. These are product strengths, not validated customer outcomes.

## Do not market yet

Exact framework/control totals; every named connector; complete AI autonomy/accuracy; all dashboard calculations; security certifications; production-grade scans/discovery; customer outcomes; free trial/pricing; and all newly added workstream artifacts. Buyer objections likely concern deployment maturity, data hosting/security, connector certification, framework coverage, implementation services and AI data handling.
