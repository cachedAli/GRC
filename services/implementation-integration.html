What we do

What our team does. And it happens on the record, not in a slide deck.

  • Deployment

    Stand the platform up the way your regulator expects: managed cloud with regional data residency, or on-premise in your own environment, air-gapped where required.

  • Configuration and customisation

    Load your frameworks, set up your organisation, roles and committees, and shape workflows, fields and reports to how you work.

  • Enterprise integration

    Connect the systems you already run, from cloud and identity to code, endpoint, ticketing and HR, and let people sign in with the identity they already have.

How an engagement runs

Five phases, two lanes. What our team does at each step, and what the platform does and keeps.

Our teamThe platform

  1. 01Plan

    Our team

    Agree the deployment model, data residency and the systems to connect.

    The platform

    Cloud (SaaS) or on-premise: the same platform and the same features either way.

    Cloud and on-premise

  2. 02Deploy

    Our team

    Provision the cloud tenant, or install in your environment.

    The platform

    Tenant-isolated by design in the cloud; your infrastructure and your control on-premise.

    Hosting options

  3. 03Configure

    Our team

    Load frameworks, organisation, roles and committees.

    The platform

    Frameworks come from the built-in library, or are imported from a document; the AI fills in the evidence each control needs.
