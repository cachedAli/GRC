What we do

What our team does. And it happens on the record, not in a slide deck.

  • Assess

    Bring in your scanner findings and enrich every CVE, check configurations against CIS Benchmarks, and surface identity gaps such as missing MFA, dormant accounts and leavers still enabled.

  • Test

    Penetration testing by our team across web applications, APIs, networks and authentication, including active OWASP testing and API security testing, inside a written and signed scope.

  • Report and fix

    Rank each finding by what is actually exploitable on that host, prioritise the fixes, and track remediation through to a retest.

Rules of engagement

Authorised scope only. Every active test runs inside a scope someone with the authority to do so has signed.

How our team works, not a platform setting. These rules are written into each engagement letter and followed by the people doing the testing. The platform records the scope, the approval and the results.

  • Signed authorisation

    No active test starts without written authorisation from someone entitled to give it.

  • Written scope

    An allowlist of the hosts, applications and APIs we may test, and a list of those we must not touch.

  • Testing windows

    Agreed dates and hours. Production is tested only where you approve it.

  • Non-destructive by default

    Proof-only checks. Nothing that deletes data, exhausts a service or changes production without your explicit approval.

  • Rate limits

    Request rates agreed in advance, so a test never becomes an outage.

  • A stop contact

    A named person on each side who can pause or stop testing at any moment.

  • Every action recorded

    What we ran, when and against which target, logged and handed over with the report.
