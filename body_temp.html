Executive Product Brief | 2026

CompliVerse AI

GRC Platform

CompliVerse AI leverages artificial intelligence to empower enterprises to scale efficiently by streamlining governance, risk management, and compliance with AI-assisted workflows.
It unifies vulnerability and asset management through structured controls and executive-ready reporting, and transforms regulatory complexity into a strategic competitive advantage.

Platform Introduction

The platform replaces fragmented GRC operations with a single integrated operating model. It connects policy design, risk assessment, and reporting in one system to reduce cycle times, improve audit readiness, and increase visibility.

Who This Serves

Built for executive leadership, compliance officers, and control owners who need fast decision support, clear accountability, and provable regulatory outcomes across multiple frameworks.

Module Summary

Governance Module

Policy lifecycle management, attestations, exceptions, and board oversight across 10 enterprise governance capabilities.

Risk Management Module

AI-supported risk identification, scoring, treatment, scenarios, and integration workflows across 10 operational capabilities.

Compliance Module

Framework ingestion, control mapping, evidence orchestration, and regulator-ready reporting across 10 compliance capabilities.
