How the work flows

Six steps. The AI works in two of them.

At each step: what you do, and what the AI does.

  • Suggests, you confirm
  • Automatic
  • No AI at this step
StepYouThe AI
  1. 01

    Step 1: Sync

    Pull accounts

    You

    Pull accounts from Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, AD / LDAP or SailPoint, or upload a file.

    The AI

    No AI at this step.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Sample

    Full, risk-based, random

    You

    Choose the method and size. The same population and size always give the same sample.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Sampling is deterministic, so an auditor can re-perform it.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Run checks

    Ten access rules

    You

    Run the rules. Each person gets findings and a 0-100 risk score.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Access rules are rules, not AI.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Certify

    AI suggestion beside each person

    You

    Decide Approve, Revoke or Exception for each person.

    The AI Suggests, you confirm

    Suggests keep, revoke or exception for each sampled person, with a reason of 22 words at most.

    Who decidesA suggestion only: it never sets the decision. An admin runs it, and the reviewer decides each person.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Report

    Verdict

    You

    Record the verdict: effective, deficient or material weakness.

    The AI Suggests, you confirm

    Turns the review’s numbers into a 4 to 6 sentence summary for the auditor.

    Who decidesGenerated on click, as a draft in the report.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Close

    Locked as audit evidence

    You

    Close the review and export CSV, Excel or PDF.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Closing is a person’s act.

Each suggestion reads the person’s e-mail, department and roles. The call passes through the data-protection gateway, which swaps names and e-mails for placeholders before the prompt leaves your server.

What the rules catch

Ten rules run on every review.

Access rules that run today
RuleCatches
IDM-01Leaver whose account is still enabled critical
IDM-04Dormant account: no sign-in for 90+ days, or never
AUTH-01Active account with no MFA registered
AUTH-02Shared or generic account (admin, svc, test, bot…)
PRIV-01Privileged role held outside IT or Security
PRIV-02Segregation-of-duties conflict: both roles of a forbidden pair
PRIV-04Privilege creep: far more roles than department peers
APRV-01Role held with no recorded approver or source
DB-01Database superuser
DB-02Default database account that can log in

Tenants switch rules on or off and change their severity. Rules, sampling and verdicts are rules, not AI.

What you get

Built for the way regulated teams actually work.

Pull every account from your directories, run access rules against them, certify each person with an AI suggestion beside them, and close the review as audit evidence.

  • Live account pulls

    Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Google Workspace, Active Directory / LDAP, SailPoint and DigitalOcean, or a CSV and Excel upload with a column mapper.

  • Access rules

    Ten rules run on every review. Switch rules on or off and change their severity for your tenant.

  • Decisions with AI beside themAI

    Approve, Revoke or Exception for each person, with the AI’s suggestion and a one-line reason next to the buttons.

  • Sampling an auditor can repeat

    Full, risk-based or random. The same population and size always yield the same sample.

  • A verdict and the evidence

    Close as effective, deficient or material weakness, and export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

Beyond the point tools

Every rule carries Secure Controls Framework IDs, so each finding maps to the access-control requirements of ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, NCA ECC and SAMA through your crosswalk.

The intelligence layer

Every AI step above runs on one intelligence layer, under the same rules as every other module.

  • Masked before it leavesAI prompts pass through a data-protection gateway: names, e-mails, hosts and IP addresses are swapped for placeholders, secrets and account numbers removed.
  • Every call on the recordAdministrators see each AI call: who asked, which feature, the tokens and the cost.
  • Budgets that alertToken usage is tracked per module against a monthly budget, with an alert before you run over.
