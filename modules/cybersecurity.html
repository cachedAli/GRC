Eight capability areas

Platform, our team, or both. Always labelled.

  • PlatformShipped in the product.
  • Platform + our teamThe platform does the work; our team runs and interprets it.
  • Our teamDelivered by CompliVerse’s security team.
  • Platform→ Vulnerability Assessment Scanner intake and hosted scans, CVE identification, CVSS scoring, severity classification and remediation recommendations. The platform does the assessment work. Our team validates and tunes it.
  • Our team→ Penetration Testing Simulated attacks on web applications, APIs, networks, authentication, authorisation and infrastructure. Our team runs the tests. The platform keeps the record.
  • Platform + our team→ OWASP Testing OWASP Top 10: injection, XSS, CSRF, SSRF, broken access control and security misconfiguration. Our team performs the tests. The platform records every requirement, pass or fail.
  • Platform + our team→ Infrastructure Security Network scanning, open-port detection, firewall assessment, server hardening and cloud security posture. The platform discovers and grades. Our team reviews what needs judgement.
  • Platform→ Configuration Audits Configuration reviews against ISO 27001, NIST, CIS Benchmarks and regulatory requirements. The platform runs the checks. Our team decides how they map to your obligations.
  • Platform + our team→ Identity & Access Security Privilege escalation risks, weak authentication, MFA gaps, excessive permissions and access-control weaknesses. Access Reviews finds the weaknesses. Our team tests how far they go.
  • Our team→ API Security Endpoint discovery, authentication flaws, rate-limiting gaps, data exposure and API vulnerabilities. Our team tests your APIs. The platform keeps the record.
  • Platform→ Risk Scoring & Reporting Risk scoring, evidence collection, exploitability assessment, remediation prioritisation and executive reports. The platform scores and reports. Our team turns it into a board story.

How the work flows

Seven steps. The AI works in three of them.

At each step: what you do, and what the AI does.

  • Suggests, you confirm
  • Automatic
  • No AI at this step
StepYouThe AI
  1. 01

    Step 1: Scope

    Authorised in writing

    You

    Sign the authorisation and agree the scope, testing windows and stop contact with our team. The platform records the scope.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Scope is agreed between people, in writing.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Bring in

    Scanners, cloud, files

    You

    Connect Tenable Nessus, Rapid7, AWS Inspector, Defender for Cloud or Google SCC, or upload Excel or CSV.

    The AI Suggests, you confirm

    Summarises an uploaded scan report in plain words.

    Who decidesAdvisory notes. Nothing changes on the findings.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Rank

    A fixed formula

    You

    Read the ranking, and the plain-English reason behind each score.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Each CVE is enriched with NVD, EPSS, CISA KEV and public-exploit data, then ranked by a fixed formula you can read. KEV-listed or likely-exploited flaws on high-value assets escalate to critical.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Map

    Findings to controls

    You

    Reject a wrong link, or decide the weaker suggestions.

    The AI Automatic

    Maps each finding to the controls in your library that address it, with a confidence and a one-line reason, or answers “patch-only”.

    Who decidesAt or above the confidence floor (medium by default) the link is made on its own and a Reject removes it. Weaker suggestions wait for a person. One decision on a weakness (CWE) applies to every finding that shares it.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Fix

    Owner, plan, SLA

    You

    Assign one owner, approve the fix plan and push a ServiceNow ticket. Your team applies the fix.

    The AI Suggests, you confirm

    Drafts a fix plan for a finding: a summary, a copy-paste fix (patch, script, IaC or config) and the rationale. Without AI configured, a template writes it.

    Who decidesPlans move recommended → approved → applied → verified, with who and when at every step.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Verify

    A re-scan proves it

    You

    Re-scan. The finding closes when the scanner no longer detects it.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Verification is a re-scan, not a judgement.

  7. 07

    Step 7: Report

    PDF, CSV, Excel, Word, HTML

    You

    Export the executive report and the register. Our team writes the narrative readout when you engage us.

    The AI

    No AI at this step. Reports are built from the record.
