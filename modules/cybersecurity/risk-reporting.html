Who delivers each capability in Risk Scoring & Reporting
CapabilityWhat happensDelivered byAI
Effective risk 0.30 × CVSS + 0.25 × EPSS + 0.20 × KEV + 0.10 × asset value + 0.15 × (business impact − 1), banded low, medium, high or critical, with a plain-English reason. Platform Not AI: a fixed formula
Escalation floor An EPSS of 0.7 or more, or a KEV listing, on a high-value asset floors the score at critical. Platform Not AI: a fixed formula
Exploitability verdict Per finding per asset: unlikely, possible or likely, from MITRE ATT&CK and CAPEC chains over stored facts. No scan, no payload. Platform Not AI: a fixed formula
Choke points Ranks the fixes that sever the most viable attack chains. Platform No AI
CTEM cycles Named scopes, such as a customer payment platform, run in cycles a person opens and closes; closing a cycle freezes its results. Platform No AI
Findings mapped to controls Each finding mapped to your controls with a confidence and a reason; weaker suggestions wait for a person. Platform Automatic
Evidence collection Findings linked to assets, controls, evidence and the risk register. Platform No AI
SLA and exceptions Due dates by severity, overdue lists, and exceptions requested, approved and revoked on the record. Platform No AI
Tickets One ServiceNow ticket per finding, its status synced back. A resolved ticket marks the fix applied; a re-scan verifies it. Platform No AI
Reports The executive vulnerability report in PDF; every register to CSV, Excel, Word, PDF or HTML. Platform No AI
Board readout The narrative report and the executive presentation. Our team No AI
Tuning Risk weights and business-impact values set with you. Our team No AI
