Who delivers each capability in Infrastructure Security
CapabilityWhat happensDelivered byAI
Network discovery Campaigns over CIDR or IP-range scopes with exclusions; excluded hosts are never touched. Up to 4,096 hosts per range, one run per campaign, and a run can be cancelled. Platform No AI
Open-port detection A host-presence probe on 27 fixed TCP ports: SMB, SSH, RDP, WinRM, web, databases, LDAP, Kubernetes and more. Not a full port scan. Platform No AI
Fingerprinting NetBIOS, reverse DNS, mDNS, SSDP and WinRM names, plus a MAC vendor lookup. Platform No AI
Authenticated inventory A WinRM or SSH sign-in to discovered hosts: operating system, installed software, antivirus and EDR posture. Platform No AI
Platform collectors Read-only collectors for Active Directory / LDAP, AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, SNMP and the main databases. Platform No AI
External attack surface From your domain: certificate transparency and keyed sources, then HTTP, security headers, TLS, DNS, SPF, DMARC and CAA checks, graded A to F. Platform No AI
Cloud security posture Findings from AWS Inspector, Defender for Cloud and Google SCC, plus CIS AWS Foundations checks. Platform No AI
Server hardening CIS Benchmark checks over SSH, WinRM and OpenSCAP, covered under configuration audits. Platform No AI
Device explanations Explains an unrecognised discovered device in plain words. Platform Suggests, you confirm
Firewall and network-device reviews Rule bases checked for over-permissive, shadowed and unused rules. Our team No AI
Full port scanning Every port on the hosts you authorise, inside the agreed windows and rate limits. Our team No AI
Cloud configuration reviews Beyond the shipped CIS rules, against your architecture and your regulator. Our team No AI

Rules of engagement

Active tests run only inside a scope you sign.

Where this area involves active testing, our team runs it under the same seven rules as every engagement. Our team

How our team runs every engagement
  • Signed authorisationSigned 30 Oct 2026
  • A written scope3 hosts · 2 apps · 1 API
  • Agreed testing windowsSun–Thu, 22:00–04:00 AST
  • Non-destructive by defaultIntrusive tests approved: none
  • Rate limitsAt most 10 requests a second per host
  • A named stop contact and procedureClient: Head of IT Operations · Ours: engagement lead
  • Every action recordedEngagement log + platform audit trail
