Who does what

Every capability, and who delivers it.

Configuration reviews against ISO 27001, NIST, CIS Benchmarks and regulatory requirements. Each row says whether it is shipped in the platform, done by our team, or both, and whether any AI is involved.

9 capabilities, each labelled with who delivers it.

Who delivers each capability in Configuration Audits
CapabilityWhat happensDelivered byAI
CIS Benchmark engine Every rule is a check with a definition, a severity and a remediation. Running checks needs its own permission. Platform No AI
Built-in rules About three dozen, hand-picked from CIS AWS Foundations v3.0 and CIS Ubuntu Linux 22.04 v2.0. Platform No AI
Import a benchmark Upload a CIS Benchmark PDF and it becomes check rows; upload a new version and it updates in place. Platform No AI
Read-only runners SSH, WinRM, OpenSCAP, AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, LDAP / AD and the main databases. Every runner is read-only by contract. Platform No AI
Mapped to your frameworks Each check maps to framework controls, so a CIS failure counts against ISO 27001, NIST and others as far as you have mapped it. Platform No AI
SaaS configuration evidence 40 live API collectors (GitHub, Okta, Microsoft 365, AWS, Cloudflare and more) mapped to SOC 2 controls. Platform No AI
Benchmark choice and mappings Picks the benchmark for a host’s operating system and suggests mappings; values it invents are rejected. Platform Suggests, you confirm
Mapping decisions How CIS checks map to ISO 27001, NIST, SAMA or NCA for your organisation. Our team No AI
Regulatory configuration reviews Settings your regulator expects that no benchmark covers. Our team No AI

PostgreSQL benchmark rules are verified against live systems; MySQL and Oracle rules are not yet, and some are held as manual checks.
