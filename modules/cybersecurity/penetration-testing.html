  • Signed authorisation

    Signed by the client’s accountable executive before any active test. No signature, no test.

    Signed 30 Oct 2026
  • A written scope

    Hosts, applications and APIs listed by name, address or path. Anything not on the list is out of scope.

    3 hosts · 2 apps · 1 API
  • Agreed testing windows

    Active testing runs only inside the windows agreed in writing.

    Sun–Thu, 22:00–04:00 AST
  • Non-destructive by default

    No denial of service, no data changes, proof-only checks. Anything intrusive needs its own written approval.

    Intrusive tests approved: none
  • Rate limits

    Request rates agreed per target, and set low for production.

    At most 10 requests a second per host
  • A named stop contact and procedure

    A named contact on each side can stop testing at any time. Testers halt on the call and confirm in writing.

    Client: Head of IT Operations · Ours: engagement lead
  • Every action recorded

    Testers log each action with its time, target and result. The platform’s audit trail records every change to scope, findings and evidence.

    Engagement log + platform audit trail

These are our team’s controls, agreed in writing before any test. The platform keeps the record: scope, findings, evidence and every change to them.
