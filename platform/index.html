The 360° loop

Not six tools bolted together. One record moving through six states.

Follow one control, Access review, all the way round.

  1. 01Frameworkdemands policy

    Five frameworks ask for the same access review.

    Every clause names the policy statement that has to exist, so the moment you pick a framework, the documents it needs are already listed against it.

    AIReads a framework you upload and fills in the controls and the evidence each one needs.

    The policy behind it is 12 days overdue.

    Access Control Policy sits in one governed library, traced to the clauses that demand it, with every version pinned. When its review slips, it shows up before anyone asks.

    AISplits each policy into statements and maps them to the controls they implement.
