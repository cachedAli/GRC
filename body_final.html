Module 1 - AI Governance

01. AI Policy Drafting and Generation

1. Overview

Creates complete framework-aligned policy drafts in minutes, replacing manual first-pass authoring.

2. Key Capabilities

3. Use Case

A bank launching PCI-DSS can generate priority policies in one day and move directly to review and approval.

4. Business Value

  • Shortens document drafting cycles by up to 80%.
