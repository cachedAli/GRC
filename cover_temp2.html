CompliVerse - AI-Powered GRC Platform

CompliVerse helps enterprises design governance, manage risk, and execute compliance at scale through AI-assisted workflows, structured controls, and executive-ready reporting.

Platform Introduction

The platform replaces fragmented GRC operations with one integrated operating model. Policy design, risk assessment, evidence collection, remediation planning, and board reporting are connected in a single system to reduce cycle time, improve audit readiness, and increase management visibility.

Who This Serves

Built for executive leadership, compliance officers, and control owners who need fast decision support, clear accountability, and provable regulatory outcomes across multiple frameworks.

Module Summary

Governance Module

Policy lifecycle management, attestations, exceptions, and board oversight across 10 enterprise governance capabilities.

Risk Management Module

AI-supported risk identification, scoring, treatment, scenarios, and integration workflows across 10 operational risk capabilities.

Compliance Module

Framework ingestion, control mapping, evidence orchestration, and regulator-ready reporting across 10 compliance capabilities.
