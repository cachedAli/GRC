Governance & Documents

11 AI jobsThe module: Governance & Documents

  • Split a policy into statements

    Automatic

    Uploaded policy → numbered policy statements

    Who approvesRuns on upload.

  • Map every statement to controls

    Automatic

    Statement + your controls → matching controls, fanned out to framework controls and evidence

    Who approvesRuns after parsing. Lock a row and later re-runs leave it alone.

  • Check a document clause by clause

    Suggests, you confirm

    Document + a framework’s clauses → the gaps, then wording that closes the one you pick

    Who approvesThe gap list is automatic; the fix is a draft that goes through review.

  • Draft a complete policy from a brief

    Suggests, you confirm

    Title, brief, your organisation profile and framework citations → outline, sections, a quality pass

    Who approvesA draft for review, like anything a person writes.

  • Suggest the policies a framework calls for

    Suggests, you confirm

    A framework → the policies, procedures and standards it needs, minus what you have

    Who approvesA list. You choose what to draft.

  • Write and edit in Compli AI Studio

    Suggests, you confirm

    Your brief or document → a planned draft; in chat, revisions, a language and requirements check, a comparison of two documents

    Who approvesEvery edit comes back as a proposal you accept. Quotes are checked against the source they cite.

  • Research the laws that apply

    Suggests, you confirm

    Subject, sector and country → applicable laws, regulations and standards, with sources listed

    Who approvesYou choose which to use.

  • Read a regulator’s circular

    Automatic

    Circular → every obligation with its reference and exact quote; a quote not found in the text is marked unverified

    Who approvesReading runs on its own; unverified quotes wait for a person to check.

  • Work out a circular’s impact

    Suggests, you confirm

    Obligations → affected policies, controls, audit observations and departments, and proposed control links

    Who approvesLinks are proposals.

  • Fill in an uploaded document’s details

    Suggests, you confirm

    File opening → title, type and description; changes → major or minor version, and why

    Who approvesNothing is saved until you confirm.

  • Draft a policy exception

    Suggests, you confirm

    The policy → justification and business impact, period and priority, affected assets, risks, compensating controls

    Who approvesRecorded only when a person submits it.

Evidence Management

5 AI jobsThe module: Evidence Management

  • Suggest the requirements a file satisfies

    Suggests, you confirm

    The file’s text → clauses of your own frameworks it appears to answer; invented IDs are dropped

    Who approvesStored as suggestions only. A person clicks Link to make each link.

  • Rate a file as audit evidence

    Automatic

    The file’s text → relevance, adequacy and audit-readiness scores, gaps and recommendations

    Who approvesRuns after upload; becomes the file’s quality score.

  • Suggest what else a file belongs to

    Suggests, you confirm

    Excerpt + candidate records → the controls, risks, assets and documents it belongs to

    Who approvesPicked only from records you hold. Nothing links until accepted.

  • Check a file against one requirement

    Suggests, you confirm

    File + requirement → satisfies or not, quoting what it saw

    Who approvesAdvisory. The reviewer decides.

  • Read scans on your own server

    Automatic

    Image or scanned PDF → text by local OCR; if nothing can be read, the image is refused

    Who approvesNot a model call: images are never sent to an outside AI.

Cybersecurity Assessment & Assurance

4 AI jobsThe module: Cybersecurity Assessment & Assurance

  • Map scanner findings to controls

    Automatic

    Finding (text, CVE, CWE, severity, asset) → controls from your library with a confidence and a reason, or “patch-only”

    Who approvesAt or above the confidence floor it links on its own and a Reject removes it; weaker suggestions wait for a person. One decision on a weakness applies to every finding that shares it.

  • Summarise scans, suggest fixes

    Suggests, you confirm

    Report or finding + asset → a summary, a suggested fix, the attack path in words

    Who approvesAdvisory. The finding’s owner decides.

  • Draft a fix plan for a finding

    Suggests, you confirm

    Finding → title, summary, a copy-paste fix and the rationale

    Who approvesApproved, then verified. Without AI configured, a template writes it instead.

  • Pick a hardening benchmark

    Suggests, you confirm

    An operating system → the CIS Benchmark that fits, and suggested mappings

    Who approvesAn operator confirms; invented values are rejected.
