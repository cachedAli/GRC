AI trust & control

Suggests or automatic

Every AI job says which it is.

  • Suggests, you confirmEvidence → requirement linksSuggested; a person clicks Link
  • Suggests, you confirmAccess review decisionsSuggested; the reviewer decides
  • Suggests, you confirmAudit observationsNot saved until accepted
  • AutomaticPolicy statements → controlsAutomatic; lock any row
  • AutomaticFindings → controlsAutomatic above a confidence floor; Reject removes

Links, decisions and edits wait for a person. The few automatic jobs say so, and can be undone.

Masked before it leaves

Names and hosts swapped, secrets cut.

Before · on your server

Revoke VPN access for mark.allen@example-bank.sa on core-db-02 (10.20.4.17). Card 4111 1111 1111 1111 is on file.

After · what the model sees

Revoke VPN access for [EMAIL_1] on [HOST_1] ([IP_1]). Card removed is on file.

Masked, then put back in the answerRemoved, never sent or restored

Illustration. If the protection check itself fails, the AI is not used: it fails closed.
