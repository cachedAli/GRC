Compliance glossary

The vocabulary of GRC, plainly defined.

The terms that run through frameworks, controls, evidence, risk and audit, without the jargon.

28 of 28 terms

A

AttestationEvidence & audit

A formal, recorded acknowledgement that a policy or control has been read, reviewed or accepted.

Governance & Documents
Audit packageEvidence & audit

The assembled set of controls and evidence handed to an assessor, built from links that already exist.

Audit Management

C

CIA triadSecurity & third parties

Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability, the three properties that information security controls protect.

CommitteeGovernance & policy

A governance body, such as a security steering committee, that owns decisions, charters and action items.

Committees & Meetings
Continuous control monitoringEvidence & audit

Automated, ongoing checks that a control keeps working between formal audits, not just at assessment time.

Integrations & Identity
ControlFrameworks & compliance

A safeguard or measure that reduces risk and satisfies a requirement, from access reviews to encryption.

Unified Control Library
Cross-framework reuseFrameworks & compliance

Using a single piece of evidence to satisfy requirements in several frameworks at once, collected only once.

Guide: evidence reuse

E

EPSS / KEVSecurity & third parties

The Exploit Prediction Scoring System and CISA’s Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue, used to prioritise fixes by real exploitability.

Risk Scoring & Reporting
EvidenceEvidence & audit

An artifact, a log, policy, configuration or report, that proves a control is designed and operating.

Evidence Management
ExceptionGovernance & policy

A documented, approved deviation from a policy or control, mapped back to what it departs from.

Governance & Documents

F

FrameworkFrameworks & compliance

A structured set of requirements and controls, such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, that an organisation aligns its programme to.

Framework library

G

Gap analysisFrameworks & compliance

A comparison of your current controls against a framework to surface exactly what is missing.

Guide: policy gap analysis

I

Inherent vs residual riskRisk

The level of risk before controls are applied (inherent) versus what remains after them (residual).

Enterprise Risk (ERM)

K

Key Risk Indicator (KRI)Risk

A metric that signals a rising level of risk as it approaches or breaches a defined threshold.

Enterprise Risk (ERM)

P

Policy lifecycleGovernance & policy

The path a document travels: draft, review, approve, publish and attest, with version history at every step.

Governance & Documents

R

RCSARisk

Risk and Control Self-Assessment: a structured review in which the business rates its own risks and control effectiveness.

Enterprise Risk (ERM)
Regulatory changeGovernance & policy

A tracked update to a law or standard that triggers a review of the affected controls and policies.

Regulatory Compliance Consulting
Remediation SLASecurity & third parties

The agreed time allowed to fix a finding, tracked from the day it is raised.

Cybersecurity Assessment & Assurance
Risk appetiteRisk

The amount and type of risk an organisation is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives.

Enterprise Risk (ERM)
Risk registerRisk

The catalogue of identified risks, each with an owner, a score and a treatment decision.

Enterprise Risk (ERM)

S

Statement of ApplicabilityFrameworks & compliance

The ISO 27001 document that records which Annex A controls apply, and the justification for any excluded.

ISO 27001

T

Tenant isolationThe CompliVerse model

Keeping each customer’s data separated in its own scope, so answers and evidence never cross tenants.

Cloud and on-premise
The linkage graphThe CompliVerse model

The connected data model that ties frameworks, policies, controls, evidence, risks and audits together as one graph.

The 360° loop
TPRMSecurity & third parties

Third-Party Risk Management: assessing and continuously monitoring vendors across their whole lifecycle.

Vendor Risk (TPRM)
TreatmentRisk

The decision on how to handle a risk: accept, mitigate, transfer or avoid it.

Enterprise Risk (ERM)
Type I / Type IIEvidence & audit

SOC 2 report kinds: a point-in-time test of control design (I) versus operating effectiveness over a period (II).

SOC 2

U

Unified control libraryFrameworks & compliance

One harmonised set of controls mapped across every framework that needs them, so you implement a control once and inherit it everywhere.

Unified Control Library

V

VulnerabilitySecurity & third parties

A weakness in a system, application or process that could be exploited by a threat.

Vulnerability Assessment
